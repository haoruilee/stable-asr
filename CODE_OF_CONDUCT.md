# Code of Conduct

Stable-ASR is intended to be a technical project for reproducible ASR and
real-time voice-system research. Contributors are expected to keep discussions
focused, factual, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Critique ideas, implementations, benchmarks, and evidence rather than people.
- Be clear when a claim is based on local experiments, upstream documentation,
  or inference.
- Respect upstream projects, licenses, and attribution requirements.
- Avoid sharing private speech data, credentials, or proprietary datasets in
  public issues, pull requests, examples, or benchmark artifacts.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Misrepresenting benchmark results, licenses, model provenance, or dataset
  provenance.
- Pressuring maintainers to merge unsafe code, hide limitations, or skip
  reproducibility checks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests;
or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

For sensitive reports, contact the maintainers privately instead of opening a
public issue.
